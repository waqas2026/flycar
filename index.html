Context

How a flying car works

Most designs fall into one of three architectures.

01

Roadable fixed-wing

Wings fold or retract for road use. Take-off requires a runway or short strip. Examples: Klein Vision AirCar, Samson Switchblade, Terrafugia Transition, AeroMobil 4.0.

02

Gyroplane / STOL

Autorotating rotor plus pusher propeller. Short take-off, no true hover. Example: PAL-V Liberty.

03

Multirotor / modular VTOL

Distributed electric propulsion for vertical lift, or a ground carrier with a separate air module. Examples: Alef Model A, ASKA A5, XPeng AeroHT.
